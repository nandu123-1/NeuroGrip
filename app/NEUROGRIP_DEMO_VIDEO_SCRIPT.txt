ACT 3: LIVE WRITING, SUB-15MS INKING & CLOSED-LOOP BIOFEEDBACK
Duration: 1:30 - 2:30 (60 Seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1:30 - 1:50]
VISUAL:
Top-down view showing both the Writing Pad and the smartphone side by side. 
The presenter takes the Smart Pen and draws smooth cursive curves across the pad. 
As the stylus touches the glass, vibrant cyan digital ink appears on the pad, 
and simultaneously mirrors onto the mobile app canvas with zero perceptible lag (<15ms).

NARRATOR (VO):
"Watch the real-time responsiveness. As the user writes, touch coordinates stream 
at 100 Hertz, mirroring strokes to the mobile app in under 15 milliseconds. 
Stroke thickness dynamically modulates based on the pen's pinch force, providing 
an authentic, responsive writing feel."

[1:50 - 2:10]
VISUAL:
Macro zoom into the Smart Pen tip and OLED display during gentle, optimal grip. 
The NeoPixel ring glows a soothing, solid emerald green. 
The OLED displays: "GRIP: 180 g | STATUS: OPTIMAL ZONE".

NARRATOR (VO):
"Notice the active biofeedback. When holding the pen within their personalized safe 
envelope—between 120 and 260 grams—the NeoPixel ring glows solid green, validating 
optimal relaxed motor control."

[2:10 - 2:30]
VISUAL:
The presenter intentionally squeezes the pen tightly (simulating hypertonic overgrip / cramp). 
Instantly, the NeoPixel ring flashes vivid crimson red, the onboard buzzer emits a sharp 
tactile haptic alert, and the OLED shows: "GRIP: 480 g | ! OVERGRIP !". 
On the phone screen, an amber alert pill flashes: "Overgrip Detected - Relax Flexor Muscles".

NARRATOR (VO):
"The moment the patient clamps down excessively—a common symptom of writer's cramp 
and dystonia—the pen immediately intervenes. Dual audio-tactile buzzing and flashing 
red LEDs cue the patient to release tension before muscle fatigue sets in."

ACT 4: PERSONALIZED CALIBRATION & THERAPIST CLINICAL ANALYTICS
Duration: 2:30 - 3:15 (45 Seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[2:30 - 2:50]
VISUAL:
Screen recording of the Companion App navigating to the "Personalized AI Baseline" screen. 
The presenter demonstrates the 30-second guided calibration routine. The user pinches 
comfortably, and the app's diagnostic model automatically calculates optimal minimum 
and maximum safety thresholds. The app sends the updated envelope straight back to the pen via BLE.

NARRATOR (VO):
"Because no two patients are identical, NeuroGrip features an adaptive calibration loop. 
In just 30 seconds, the system profiles individual grip variance and automatically pushes 
personalized minimum and maximum force thresholds directly to the pen's firmware."

[2:50 - 3:15]
VISUAL:
Presenter taps the "Therapist Analytics" tab on the tablet/phone. 
A sleek, high-contrast dashboard appears with interactive charts:
- Real-time Grip Force Deviation (GFD) curve
- IMU Tremor Power Spectrum isolating the 4–8 Hz Parkinsonian peak
- Micrographia Decay Slope tracking font size reduction over time
- Cumulative Neuromuscular Stability Score (e.g., 88/100)

NARRATOR (VO):
"For clinicians and occupational therapists, the app provides deep, quantitative motor 
biomarkers. We isolate involuntary tremors in the critical 4 to 8 Hertz Parkinsonian band, 
quantify micrographia decay, and track session-by-session neuromuscular stability scores."
